Biography & Overview

Dr. Ahmed Hassan is a senior Orthodontist specialist with over 9 years of clinical expertise. He excels in correcting misaligned teeth and jaw structure using modern metallic braces and invisible aligners (Invisalign). Dr. Ahmed has successfully transformed over 1,500 smiles for adults and teenagers.
